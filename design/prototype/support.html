How does SmartGullak decide if I can afford something?expand_more

We project your next 90 days of cashflow from your reconciled statements — salary, freelance receipts, EMIs, subscriptions and seasonal spends — then subtract the purchase and check that your emergency floor stays intact. The Ask screen shows the full decision waterfall.

Which banks are supported?expand_more

HDFC, ICICI and Kotak statements (PDF, CSV and XLS) are parsed automatically. Other banks can be added with the generic CSV template on the Statements page.

Why was a transaction flagged?expand_more

Flags appear for likely duplicates, unusually large amounts and first-time merchants. Resolve them on the Flags page so the forecast uses clean data.

Is my data shared with anyone?expand_more

No. Statements are parsed on your device and stored encrypted. Sensitive actions such as exporting or deleting data require your password and your SmartGullak NFC card.

What is the Track Record?expand_more

An append-only, hash-chained log of every forecast and decision SmartGullak has made, so you can verify nothing was changed after the fact.

I lost my SmartGullak card. What now?expand_more

Go to Privacy & Data, choose Replace card, and verify by email. Your old card is revoked immediately.
